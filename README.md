# Science Pages

Interactive science pages, published with GitHub Pages at https://werdna1976.github.io/Science-Pages/

- [Ohm's Law Lab](https://werdna1976.github.io/Science-Pages/ohms-law/): light bulbs in series and parallel
- [The Dairy Lab: Food Science](https://werdna1976.github.io/Science-Pages/food-science/): Science Olympiad Food Science study guide with ice cream, curd, and butter exhibits
- [The Tide Pool Lab: Water Quality](https://werdna1976.github.io/Science-Pages/water-quality/): Science Olympiad Water Quality study guide (marine and estuary year) with estuary, coral bleaching, dissolved oxygen, and salinometer exhibits
- [The Hang Time Lab: Elastic Launched Glider](https://werdna1976.github.io/Science-Pages/elastic-glider/): Science Olympiad Elastic Launched Glider study guide with launch, glide, balance, and turning exhibits, a practice flight log, and a build and trim guide
- [The Spark Lab: Circuit Lab](https://werdna1976.github.io/Science-Pages/circuit-lab/): Science Olympiad Circuit Lab study guide (2027) with Ohm's law, circuit, LED, logic gate, adder, Coulomb's law, multimeter, and color code exhibits
- [The Breadboard Bench: Circuit Lab](https://werdna1976.github.io/Science-Pages/circuit-lab/breadboard/): interactive breadboard with LEDs, switches, and 74HC logic chips, a live multimeter, and build and fix-it challenges
